Nydegger Online Automatic Intelligence Scale - Form 2

NOAIS - Form 2 is an untimed, high range intelligence battery consisting of 115 items, designed to measure cognitive ability across a broad range, from approximately the population average to exceptionally high levels (+6 standard deviations).

The battery consists of 115 items distributed across five reasoning domains: Numerical, Verbal, Abstract, Logical and Spatial. Within each subtest, items are arranged in approximately increasing order of difficulty.

The five subtests may be completed independently and in any order. Your progress is saved automatically, allowing you to return later if necessary.

During each subtest your raw score is displayed. After completing a subtest, an estimated standardized score becomes available. Once all five subtests have been completed, an overall estimated I.Q. score (mean = 100, standard deviation = 15) is calculated.

NOAIS - Form 2 is a battery following the same general philosophy as NAITOR and NEAITO.

The battery is free of charge for authenticated candidates. Unauthenticated candidates may participate by purchasing access to individual subtests.

For optimal usability, it is recommended to complete the spatial subtest on a computer rather than on a smartphone.
