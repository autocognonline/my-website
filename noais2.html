Nydegger Online Automatic Intelligence Scale - Form 2

NOAIS - Form 2 is an untimed, high-range intelligence battery consisting of 115 items, designed to measure cognitive ability across a broad range, from approximately the population average to exceptionally high levels of ability (+6 standard deviations).

The battery consists of 115 items distributed across five reasoning domains: Numerical, Verbal, Abstract, Logical and Spatial. Within each subtest, items are arranged in approximately increasing order of difficulty.

The five subtests may be completed independently and in any order. Your progress is saved automatically, allowing you to return later if necessary.

During each subtest your raw score is displayed. After completing a subtest, an estimated standardized score becomes available. Once all five subtests have been completed, an overall estimated IQ score (Wechsler scale, mean = 100, SD = 15) is calculated.

NOAIS - Form 2 is a new battery following the same general philosophy as NAITOR and NEAITO.

Authenticated candidates may complete the battery free of charge. Unauthenticated candidates may participate by purchasing access to individual subtests.

It is recommended to take the spatial subtest on a computer browsers not on a smartphone.
