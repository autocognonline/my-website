Nydegger Online Automatic Cognitive Test

The NOACT is a computerized high range cognitive assessment consisting of 50 items covering numerical, verbal, logical, and abstract reasoning.

Items are presented in approximate order of increasing difficulty and are designed to assess abilities approximately from one to five standard deviations above the average.

The test has no time limit.

Scores are reported on the Wechsler I.Q. scale (mean = 100, standard deviation = 15).

The NOACT is an online application that performs best on modern desktop computer browsers.

Participation is free of charge for authenticated candidates.

Please do not share, discuss, publish, or reproduce any test items or solutions.

The use of AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, etc.) for solving, explaining, analysing, or verifying answers is strictly prohibited.

External reference resources such as dictionaries, calculators, Wikipedia and Wolfram Alpha may be used.
