Previous IQ Test Scores

Share your previous scores

If you have previously taken other IQ tests, you can submit your earlier scores through this interface.

These submissions help improve the quality of the statistical reports on this website, including correlations, g-loadings, norms, and various comparisons between tests.

After logging in, you can also see which scores are already recorded for your email address, so you can check for missing or incorrect entries.

  • Only first and second attempts are accepted.
  • Each submission must include clear proof of the score.
  • All submitted scores are reviewed manually before validation so please be patient.
Every 10 validated submitted scores allow you to request one free score certificate for a test available on this website.
