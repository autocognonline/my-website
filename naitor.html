Nydegger Automatic Intelligence Test Online Revised

The NAITOR is an online, untimed and computerized IQ assessment consisting of 80 items designed to measure cognitive ability beyond the range of standard IQ tests.

It is a revised version of the NAIT: some items have been updated, others have been added or removed, and the test is now fully accessible online.

The items cover numerical, verbal, logical, abstract, and spatial reasoning and are approximately arranged in order of increasing difficulty.

Scores are reported on the Wechsler IQ scale (mean 100, standard deviation 15).

The test is primarily intended for individuals aged 18 and above.

While not a replacement for professionally administered psychological evaluations, the NAITOR is intended as a complementary tool for exploring cognitive ability.

Note: For candidates who had already started or finished the NAIT, your progress has been carried over to the NAITOR, and any certificates remain valid.
