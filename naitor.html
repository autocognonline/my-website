Nydegger Automatic Intelligence Test Online Revised

The NAITOR is an online, untimed and computerized I.Q. assessment consisting of 80 items designed to measure cognitive ability beyond the range of standard I.Q. tests.

It is a revised version of the NAIT: some items have been updated, others have been added or removed, and the test is now fully accessible online.

The items cover numerical, verbal, logical, abstract, and spatial reasoning and are approximately arranged in order of increasing difficulty.

Scores are reported on the Wechsler I.Q. scale (mean = 100, standard deviation = 15).

The test is primarily intended for individuals aged 18 and above.

Attention: This test requires a significant amount of time to complete. It is recommended to spend at least 15 minutes on items that seem too difficult. Please take your time and give each item careful consideration before moving on.

Participation is free of charge for authenticated candidates.

Please do not share, discuss, publish, or reproduce any test items or solutions.

The use of AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, etc.) for solving, explaining, analysing, or verifying answers is strictly prohibited.

External reference resources such as dictionaries, calculators, Wikipedia and Wolfram Alpha may be used.
