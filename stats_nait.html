Statistics of NAIT and its subtests

NAIT, developed in mai 2025, was the predecessor of NAITOR.

It was an untimed, 120-item heterogeneous test that included numerical, verbal, logical, abstract, and spatial questions.

Below you can find the final statistics from the test before it was officially discontinued in march 2026.
