Cognimetrica Score Calculator

This calculator allows you to compute Cognimetrica scores for high range tests.

Scores are calculated as the arithmetic mean of submitted scores. The difference between the highest and lowest scores must not exceed 10 I.Q. points. No individual item type may account for more than 50% of submitted tests. Number of authors is limited to 10.
