Authentication Procedure

Individuals below the age of 16 are not allowed.

To verify your identity, please follow the steps below:

All new users must submit a valid identity document (ID, passport, driving license) using the form below.

If any of the following apply to you, your identity document only needs to show your full name and date of birth (a facial photograph on the document is not required):

• You joined the Glia Society before 2024.

• You are a member of the Cognimetrica Society.

• You previously completed NIT – Form 1 (full test).

If none of the above apply, your identity document must also include a clearly visible photograph of your face.

In addition, you must submit a recent photograph of yourself. This is required to confirm that the person submitting the information is the same individual named on the identity document.

If you prefer not to submit a facial photograph, a one-time fee of 30 EURO must be paid using the PayPal button below.

This information is collected to prevent individuals from retaking tests under a false identity. It will not be shared with any third party unless there is a strong reason to suspect fraudulent activity.

Under normal circumstances, authentication is completed within 3 days (typically within 1 business day). During vacation periods, processing may take up to 7 days.

Authentication might be rejected if fraudulent activity is suspected even if all the requested data has been provided.
