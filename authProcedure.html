Authentication Procedure

To verify your identity, please follow the steps below:

All new users must submit a valid identity document (ID, passport, driving license), by sending it directly to this email address.

Your full name and date of birth must be clearly visible on the document.

If you are a member of the Glia Society and joined before 2024, this information is sufficient. If you are not a member, the document must also include a visible photograph of your face.

In addition, you must submit a recent photograph of yourself. This is required to confirm that the person submitting the information is the same individual named on the identity document.

If you prefer not to submit a facial photograph, a one-time fee of 50 CHF must be paid using the PayPal button below.

Pay with PayPal

This information is collected to prevent individuals from retaking tests under a false identity. It will not be shared with any third party unless there is a strong reason to suspect fraudulent activity.

Procedure visual summary

Under normal circumstances, authentication is completed within 3 days (typically within 1 business day). During vacation periods, processing may take up to 7 days.

Authentication might be rejected if fraudulent activity is suspected even if all the data requested as been provided.
