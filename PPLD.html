Privacy Policy and Legal Disclaimer

Last updated: 12 March 2026

This page explains how personal data is collected and used, and outlines the limitations and conditions of using Orbifoldanswers, an online cognitive testing platform.

By creating an account, authenticating, or participating in the test(s), you agree to the terms described in this document.

1. About This Project

Orbifoldanswers is an independent project created, developed and maintained by a single individual (Marc-André Nydegger).

The creator of this project has a background in mechanics and computer science and is not a licensed psychologist, psychiatrist, or medical professional.

This project is not affiliated with any university, psychological association, research institution, or medical organization.

The cognitive tests and all supporting software on this website were designed and implemented independently by the creator of the project

2. Purpose of the Tests

The cognitive tests are intended for research, analytical, educational, or entertainment purposes.

The results produced by these tests:

  • Do not constitute a clinical psychological evaluation
  • Should not be interpreted as a medical diagnosis
  • Should not be used for employment, academic, or medical decisions

Test standard scores are approximate estimates based on the current norming methodology.

No guarantee is made regarding the accuracy, validity, or scientific reliability of the results.

3. Personal Data Collected

During authentication or participation in the test(s), the following personal information is collected:

3.1. Information provided by users

  • Name
  • Age
  • Country of residence
  • Sex
  • Email address

This information is requested to:

  • Authenticate participants (main reason)
  • Generate demographic statistics
  • Analyze performance trends across populations
  • Improve test calibration

Most features of the platform require this information.

3.2. Test Data

During the test, the platform records:

  • Items solved and failed
  • Completion times
  • Interaction patterns
  • Calculated scores or rankings

This data is used to generate test results and improve the platform.

3.3. Technical Information

When accessing the website or interacting with the test apps, certain technical data may be automatically collected, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Session identifiers
  • Timestamps of access

This information is used for security, fraud prevention, and system operation.

For users located in jurisdictions covered by data protection regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), personal data is processed under the following legal bases:

  • User consent : when you voluntarily provide information and participate in the test
  • Legitimate interest : operating and improving the platform, maintaining test integrity, and preventing abuse
  • Legal obligations : when disclosure is required by law

5. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Data may only be disclosed in limited situations, which are:

If hosting or infrastructure providers process data, they do so solely for technical purposes and under appropriate safeguards.

6. Data Retention

Personal data is stored only for as long as reasonably necessary for:

Statistical or research datasets may be anonymized or aggregated and retained for longer periods.

10. Fraud Prevention and Test Integrity

The platform and test author may monitor behavior to detect potential attempts to manipulate results, including:

  • Multiple accounts creation attempts
  • Sharing or reproducing test items
  • Sharing items answers
  • Candidates collaboration with third parties
  • Abnormal response patterns

If manipulation or fraud is suspected, the platform may:

  • Invalidate test scores
  • Permanently delete test data
  • Suspend or restrict accounts
  • Report any anti-social behavior to third parties

11. Anti-Item Leakage Policy

The integrity of cognitive tests depends on the confidentiality of test items.

Users are strictly prohibited from reproducing, distributing, publishing, or sharing any part of the test content, including:

  • Test questions or visual items
  • Correct answers
  • Partial screenshots of test content
  • Descriptions of test items intended to reveal their structure or solution

This prohibition applies to all forms of distribution, including but not limited to:

  • Online forums
  • Social media
  • Messaging platforms
  • Video recordings or streaming
  • Blogs or articles

Unauthorized disclosure of test items may compromise the validity of the assessment and the fairness of results.

If test item leakage is detected, the platform may modify or remove affected items and take action against the responsible accounts.

12. Score Invalidation Policy

The platform reserves the right to invalidate, remove, or refuse to recognize test scores if the integrity of the testing process is compromised.

Scores may be invalidated in situations including, but not limited to:

  • Evidence of cheating or external assistance
  • Multiple attempts using different accounts
  • Use of automated tools or scripts
  • Access to leaked or previously disclosed test items
  • Abnormal response behavior indicating manipulation
  • Technical anomalies that invalidate the testing conditions

Invalidated scores will be removed from rankings, statistics, and user profiles.

The platform reserves the right to make final determinations regarding the validity of test results.

18. Normative Data and Score Calculation

Standard scores and percentile rankings produced by the platform are calculated using normative data derived from completed test submissions.

Only data from participants who have fully completed the test and explicitly submitted their results as finished are included in the computation of norms.

Test attempts that are abandoned, interrupted, incomplete, or not formally submitted as finished are excluded from normative datasets.

This approach is intended to improve the reliability of statistical comparisons between participants and reduce distortions caused by incomplete testing sessions.

Normative statistics are recalculated periodically as additional completed submissions become available.
