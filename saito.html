Short Automatic Intelligence Test Online

SAITO is an untimed, computerized, high range cognitive test consisting of 30 items covering numerical, abstract, verbal, logical, and spatial reasoning. Item difficulty increases progressively throughout the test.

SAITO is intended both as a formal evaluation and as a demonstration test for individuals who are unfamiliar with high range cognitive tests or who wish to become familiar with the format before attempting the other tests available on this website.

The overall difficulty is generally lower and the ceiling lower than in the other tests offered here.

Candidates do not need to be authenticated to take SAITO; verifying an email address is sufficient.

Your raw score is displayed during the test and the estimated I.Q. score (mean = 100, standard deviation = 15) becomes available after completion.

SAITO follows the same general format and item style as NOACT and NOFRAT, but in a shorter and more condensed version.

Note: Authenticated candidates can generate a score certificate that is automatically updated whenever the norms change.
