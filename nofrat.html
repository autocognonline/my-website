Nydegger Online Formal Reasoning Automatic Test

NOFRAT is an untimed and computerized, high-range cognitive assessment consisting of 55 items spanning numerical, abstract, and spatial reasoning.

It is designed as a culture-fair alternative to NAITOR and NEAITO, requiring only minimal prior knowledge to solve.

Items are presented in an approximate order of increasing difficulty and are intended to assess abilities ranging from about one to six standard deviations above the average.

NOFRAT is part of NOAIS - Form 1.

NOFRAT is an online application that performs best on modern desktop browsers.

Participation is subject to a fee.

Please do not share, discuss, publish, or reproduce any test items or solutions.

The use of AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, etc.) for solving, explaining, analysing, or verifying answers is strictly prohibited.

External resources such as calculators and Wolfram Alpha may be used.
