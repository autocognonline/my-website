Nydegger Online Cognitive Timed Automatic Examination

NOCTÆ is an online, timed I.Q. test consisting of 48 items. It is designed to measure non-verbal cognitive ability beyond the range of most standard I.Q. tests.

The test is culture-fair and requires minimal prior knowledge.

The items assess numerical, abstract, and spatial reasoning, and are approximately arranged in order of increasing difficulty.

The test is intended to measure cognitive ability approximately from one to five standard deviations above the average.

The time limit is 240 minutes (4 hours).

The test is suitable for individuals with limited English proficiency aged 18 years and older.

Scores are reported on the Wechsler I.Q. scale (mean = 100, standard deviation = 15).

Note: This test has a visual dark mode.

Before starting:
• Ensure access to a stable internet connection
• Use a quiet environment with minimal interruptions
• Allow sufficient time to complete the test in a single session
• Use a device with an adequately sized screen (desktop or tablet recommended)
• Be in a rested and focused state

For individuals uncertain about the suitability of a timed test, it may be useful to complete SAITO beforehand. Completion within approximately 5 hours of active problem-solving (even if spread over multiple days) suggests that NOCTÆ will be manageable.

Participation is free of charge for authenticated candidates.

Please do not share, discuss, publish, or reproduce any test items or solutions.

The use of AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, etc.) for solving, explaining, analysing, or verifying answers is strictly prohibited.

External resources such as calculators and Wolfram Alpha may be used.
