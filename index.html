Introduction

On this website, you will find experimental intelligence tests that I have created or co-authored.

The project originally ran from 2009 to 2015 and was revived in 2025 in a new format, featuring improved test items, automated scoring and more detailed statistics.

All tests on this website use automated scoring, allowing immediate results while ensuring consistent and objective evaluation. This contrasts with many high range tests that are manually graded, where receiving feedback may take days or even weeks.

Compared to conventional I.Q. tests, the tests found on this website are intentionally more challenging and are designed to provide a higher measurement ceiling.

As a result, most tests require several hours to complete and are intended for motivated individuals who enjoy working on difficult problems.

However, these tests are not a substitute for professionally administered psychological assessments, as they do not assess all of the constructs evaluated by such assessments. Consequently, they are not intended for use in psychological or medical diagnosis.

Furthermore, because data collection and validation are ongoing, the standard scores provided should be interpreted as provisional estimates that might vary by a significant amount rather than accurate measures of cognitive ability.

The tests on this website are intended for individuals aged 16 or older and can all be taken free of charge.

Revocations are only allowed if there are attempts remaining on at least one item and can only be done once per test.
