Computerized high range cognitive tests

On this website, you will find experimental intelligence tests that I have created or co-authored.

This project began in 2009 and was active until 2015. It was revived in 2025 in a new format, featuring improved test items and updated scoring procedures.

The tests were developed based on prior item designs and experience derived from item analysis and are refined as additional data is collected.

Standard scores are estimated through statistical analysis and are adjusted as the dataset grows and norming methods improves.

These test are designed to be substantially more challenging than conventional IQ tests. With a higher measurement ceiling, they aim to assess exceptional cognitive levels.

Most tests require several hours to complete and are intended for motivated individuals who enjoy complex reasoning and difficult problem-solving tasks.

All tests here have no time limit. This power test design allows participants to focus entirely on reasoning processes rather than speed, and may therefore reduce the influence of factors such as age, fatigue, or temporary health conditions.

Unlike many high range IQ tests that require manual grading, all tests on this site use automated scoring. This allows results to be delivered immediately while ensuring consistent and objective evaluation.

Although these tests are not a substitute for professionally administered psychological assessments, they may serve as an exploratory tool for individuals interested in evaluating their cognitive ability.

Scores should be interpreted as estimates rather than definitive measures of intelligence until sufficient data has been collected and the validity becomes apparent.

The tests are intended for individuals aged 16 and older.

Whether you are seeking an intellectual challenge, pursuing a hobby, or simply curious about your cognitive abilities, all tests on this website are available free of charge.

Standalone tests

SAITO

A 30-item, untimed, online computerized test assessing numerical, verbal, logical, abstract, and spatial reasoning. Designed as a brief introduction and demonstration, this test is recommended as a starting point for those new to high range tests.
Overall Test Complexity: medium

NAITOR

A 80-item, untimed, online computerized test covering numerical, verbal, logical, abstract, and spatial reasoning. This is a revised version of the NAIT. If you are unsure which test to take, this is the recommended option.
Overall Test Complexity: medium

NEAITO

A 60-item, untimed, online computerized test covering the same domains as NAITOR, but shorter, harder, and with an increased ceiling for higher IQ measurement.
Overall Test Complexity: very high

NOACT

A 50-item, untimed, online computerized test covering numerical, verbal, logical, and abstract reasoning with progressive difficulty.
Overall Test Complexity: high

NOFRAT

A 55-item, untimed, online computerized culture-fair test assessing numerical, abstract, and spatial reasoning with progressive difficulty.
Overall Test Complexity: very high

OVAT 33

A 33-item, untimed, online computerized, high range verbal subtest of NOAIS.
Overall Test Complexity: medium

SAT

A 36-item, high-range spatial test, co-created with Andrei Udriște.
Overall Test Complexity: high

Combined tests

NOAIS

A 88-item, untimed, online computerized high range test covering numerical, verbal, abstract and spatial reasoning with progressive difficulty. It is the fusion of NOFRAT and OVAT33.
Overall Test Complexity: high
