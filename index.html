Introduction

On this website, you will find experimental intelligence tests that I have created or co-authored.

The project originally ran from 2009 to 2015 and was revived in 2025 in a new format, featuring improved test items and updated scoring procedures.

Compared to conventional IQ tests, these assessments are intentionally more challenging and are designed to provide a higher measurement ceiling, making them particularly suitable for evaluating exceptional cognitive ability.

As a result, most tests require several hours to complete and are intended for motivated individuals who enjoy working on difficult problems.

To support this format, all tests use automated scoring, allowing immediate results while ensuring consistent and objective evaluation. This contrasts with many high range tests that are manually graded, where receiving feedback may take days or even weeks.

However, these tests are not a substitute for professionally administered psychological assessments. They are not designed to measure exactly the same constructs and cannot be used for psychological or medical diagnosis.

Furthermore, because data collection and validation are ongoing, standard scores should be interpreted as provisional estimates that might vary by a significant amount rather than definitive measures of intelligence.

The tests are intended for individuals aged 16 and older.

Whether you are seeking an intellectual challenge, pursuing a hobby, or simply curious about your abilities, you can attempt the tests available on this website free of charge.
