Introduction

On this website, you will find experimental intelligence tests that I have created or co-authored.

The project originally ran from 2009 to 2015 and was revived in 2025 in a new format, featuring improved test items and updated scoring procedures.

All tests are developed through iterative item design informed by prior experience and statistical analysis. As additional data is collected, the tests are revised to improve accuracy and reliability.

Standard scores are estimated using statistical methods and are adjusted over time as the dataset grows and norming procedures become more robust.

Compared to conventional IQ tests, these assessments are intentionally more challenging and aim to provide a higher measurement ceiling, making them suitable for evaluating exceptional cognitive ability.

Most tests require several hours to complete and are intended for motivated individuals who enjoy solving difficult puzzles.

Most assessments here have no time limit. This “power test” approach emphasizes depth of reasoning over speed and may reduce the influence of external factors such as age, fatigue, or temporary health conditions.

All tests use automated scoring, allowing immediate results while ensuring consistent and objective evaluation, unlike many high-range tests that are manually graded and where getting feedback might take days or weeks.

Although these tests are not a substitute for professionally administered psychological assessments, they can serve as an exploratory tool for individuals interested in assessing their cognitive abilities.

Standard scores should be interpreted as provisional estimates rather than definitive measures of intelligence while data collection and validation are ongoing.

The tests are intended for individuals aged 16 and older.

Whether you are seeking an intellectual challenge, pursuing a hobby, or simply curious about your abilities, you are welcome to explore the tests available on this website free of charge.

Standalone tests

SAITO

A 30-item, untimed, online computerized test assessing numerical, verbal, logical, abstract, and spatial reasoning. Designed as a brief introduction and demonstration, this test is recommended as a starting point for those new to high range tests.
Overall Test Complexity: medium

NOCTÆ

A 48-item, timed, online computerized culture-fair test assessing numerical, abstract, and spatial reasoning with progressive difficulty.
Overall Test Complexity: high

NAITOR

A 80-item, untimed, online computerized test covering numerical, verbal, logical, abstract, and spatial reasoning. This is a revised version of the NAIT. If you are unsure which test to take, this is the recommended option.
Overall Test Complexity: medium

NEAITO

A 60-item, untimed, online computerized test covering the same domains as NAITOR, but shorter, harder, and with an increased ceiling for higher IQ measurement.
Overall Test Complexity: very high

NOACT

A 50-item, untimed, online computerized test covering numerical, verbal, logical, and abstract reasoning with progressive difficulty.
Overall Test Complexity: high

NOFRAT

A 55-item, untimed, online computerized culture-fair test assessing numerical, abstract, and spatial reasoning with progressive difficulty.
Overall Test Complexity: very high

OVAT 33

A 33-item, untimed, online computerized, high range verbal subtest of NOAIS.
Overall Test Complexity: medium

SAT

A 36-item, high-range spatial test, co-created with Andrei Udriște.
Overall Test Complexity: high

Combined tests

NOAIS

A 88-item, untimed, online computerized high range test covering numerical, verbal, abstract and spatial reasoning with progressive difficulty. It is the fusion of NOFRAT and OVAT33.
Overall Test Complexity: high
