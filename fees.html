Fees and Certificates

Non-public tests require a fee before answers can be submitted. Authenticated candidates can access the test and view its items, but answering is disabled until the required fee has been paid.

SAITO and CAISO can be accessed and answered free of charge, regardless of authentication status. However, authentication is required to obtain a certificate.

For NOCTÆ, payment is required before candidates can access the test items and submit answers.

For NOAIS - Form 2, candidates may also purchase access to individual subtests separately.

Certificates become available after completion of the corresponding test. Certificates must be generated and downloaded through the application; they are not sent by email.

Certificates are automatically updated when the applicable norms change. Additional statistical information, such as confidence intervals, may be included in future versions when the available data allow it.

Note: Candidates who took a test free of charge before fees were introduced can see a certificate only if they previously had access to it. Otherwise, the answering fee must be paid to access the certificate.
