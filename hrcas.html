High Range Cognitive Ability Scale

HRCAS is an untimed, high-range cognitive ability battery consisting of 86 items. It is designed to measure cognitive ability across the range extending from approximately one to five standard deviations above the population mean.

The battery includes numerical, verbal, abstract, logical, and spatial reasoning items, arranged approximately in order of increasing difficulty.

HRCAS consists of two independent (sub)tests: NOACT and SAT.

NOACT assesses cognitive ability through numerical, verbal, abstract, and logical reasoning tasks. It is suitable for candidates who are proficient in English.

SAT is the spatial reasoning subtest of HRCAS.

For the most comprehensive assessment of cognitive ability, candidates are encouraged to complete both NOACT and SAT.
