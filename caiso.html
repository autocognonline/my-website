Concise Automatic Intelligence Scale Online

CAISO is an untimed, high range intelligence scale consisting of 56 items.

CAISO extends SAITO by adding (much) more difficult items, raising the scale's ceiling to approximately 6 standard deviations above the population mean.

The test assesses numerical, verbal, abstract, logical and spatial reasoning, with items arranged approximately in order of increasing difficulty.

Candidates must have completed SAITO before taking CAISO.

The first 30 items of CAISO are the same 30 items already completed as part of SAITO. Your existing SAITO results for these 30 items are carried over to CAISO, so you will not see or take these items again. CAISO therefore begins with the additional 26 items beyond SAITO.

CAISO is recommended for candidates who achieved at least one ceiling score on any SAITO subtest and/or scored above 140 I.Q. on SAITO.

As with SAITO, CAISO is open to all candidates regardless of authentication status. Candidates simply need to have completed SAITO at least 48 hours earlier if they are not authenticated. This waiting period helps prevent abuse.

Your raw score is displayed during the test and the estimated I.Q. score (mean = 100, standard deviation = 15) becomes available after completion.
