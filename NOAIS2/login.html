Nydegger Online Automatic Intelligence Scale - Form 2

General Instructions

Spatial, Abstract and Logical Subtests

In these subtests, items are answered one at a time. For each item, you will either submit an alphanumeric answer or a drawing before moving to another item.

Each item allows 3 attempts. After 3 incorrect attempts, the item becomes unavailable.

How to use the Spatial Subtest's canvas PDF

Numerical and Verbal Subtests

In these subtests, you will enter your answers in a form and submit all answers at the same time.

After each submission, incorrectly answered items remain available. You have 3 total submissions.

A subtest ends when no questions remain available, or when you click the “End Test” button.
During each subtest your raw score is displayed. After completing a subtest, an estimated standardized score becomes available. Once all five subtests have been completed, an overall estimated IQ score is calculated.
