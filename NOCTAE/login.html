Nydegger Online Cognitive Timed Automatic Examination

Instructions:
Items are presented in roughly increasing order of difficulty, but you may answer them in any order you choose.
You will answer each item as it appears; there is no option to submit all answers at once.
Each item allows 3 attempts. Every incorrect answer uses one attempt.
An item becomes unavailable once all attempts have been used.
The test ends when there are no more questions available to answer or when time runs out.
For spatial items, you will be provided with a canvas on which you must draw your answer.
All spatial answers are matrices composed of black and white cells.
Place your finger on a cell and press (smartphone) or right-click (computer) to change its color.
You have 240 minutes (4 hours) to complete the test, starting from the moment you click the “Start Test” button. The timer will continue running even if you close the application, so please ensure you will not be interrupted during this period.

The test can only be started after paying the fee.
